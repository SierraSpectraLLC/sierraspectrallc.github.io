Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The things labs usually want to know before they call us in.

Which manufacturers and instruments do you service?

We work across Sciex, Agilent, Waters, Shimadzu, Thermo Fisher, Bruker, and PerkinElmer platforms, covering triple quadrupole MS, Q-TOF, ion trap, Orbitrap, GC-MS, ICP-MS, and single quadrupole systems, along with HPLC and UHPLC. If your instrument isn't in our platform list, contact us anyway; our experience extends to many additional configurations.

How quickly can you respond when an instrument goes down?

For critical failures we provide same-day or next-day onsite support depending on your location and parts availability. Contract clients also have access to weekend emergency service. Calling (833) 574-3772 is faster than the form if your instrument is down right now.

Do you offer service contracts, or is it all per-visit?

Both. You can bring us in for one-off repairs and preventative maintenance, or set up a service agreement. Contracts range from basic coverage to comprehensive plans with dedicated engineer support, and are built around your instrument mix, usage, and budget rather than a fixed tier list.

What does a preventative maintenance visit include?

PM scope depends on the platform, but generally covers inspection and replacement of consumable and wear components, cleaning of the source and flow path, calibration and performance verification, and a written record of what was done. We build PM schedules around your actual usage patterns rather than a generic annual interval.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Roseville, CA and serve the greater Sacramento region and Northern California. See our service area for the cities we visit regularly. Coverage often extends further for contract clients and scheduled work.

How do I get a quote?

Send us the instrument make, model, and a description of the issue or the service you need using the contact form, or call us directly. We'll confirm scope and timing and send a written quote before any work begins.

Still have a question? Ask us directly.
